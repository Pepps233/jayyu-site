I recently joined Crcle AI as a software engineer intern.
This will be my first experience working as a software engineer, alongside a team of industrial designers, developers, and engineers.

During the interview, the recruiter was interested in my ASL hand sign translator project.
I had the chance to explain the project in detail, and the conversation went well.
It was encouraging to see the preparation I had put into it help me talk through my work.

My work at Crcle will focus on building user context features and refining the large language model that users will interact with when the product launches.
The team's goal is to release mobile and desktop apps by the end of 2025.

I’m looking forward to working with the team and seeing how these parts come together in the product.
