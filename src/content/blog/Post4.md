I recently joined Hack the Future at Purdue University after completing its application and interview process.
Hack the Future, or HTF, is a student organization that develops software for nonprofit organizations around the world.
Its work helps these organizations improve their operations and reach more people.

The application involved a résumé-style background review followed by an interview with one of the executive members.
The interviewer was friendly and patient, and I had the chance to talk about my interests and past projects.
Our conversation also helped me understand the organization's mission and how its teams work.

I appreciated the care the team put into the application process.
I’m looking forward to working with other students and contributing to projects that support nonprofit organizations.
